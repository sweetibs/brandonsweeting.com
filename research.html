Research

Weighted inequalities and singular integrals

My research is in real-variable harmonic analysis, at the intersection of Calderón–Zygmund theory and weighted norm inequalities. I study singular integral operators, maximal operators, and the Bergman projection in both the scalar and matrix weighted settings, using dyadic and Bellman function techniques.

01

Multiplier weak-type inequalities

Weak-type estimates in which the weight acts as a multiplier on the operator rather than as a measure, introduced by Muckenhoupt and Wheeden. Recent work characterizes the weights for the maximal operator when p > 1; ongoing work with D. Cruz-Uribe and K. Moen treats two weights.

02

Multilinear operators

Sharp weighted weak-type bounds for multilinear singular integrals, valid for any multilinear operator with a sparse form domination, in both the change of measure and multiplier settings.

03

The Bergman projection

Weak-type estimates for the Bergman projection on planar domains, and harmonic analysis techniques for operators in several complex variables.

04

T(b) theorems

T(b) theorems for singular integral operators on spaces of homogeneous type and in the Dunkl setting.

05

Bellman functions

Sharp dyadic estimates for A2 weights via non-infinitesimal Bellman majorants, where the extremal functions do not arise from a PDE.

06

Wavelets and signals

A longer-term interest in wavelet analysis and its applications to signal processing and the biomedical sciences.
