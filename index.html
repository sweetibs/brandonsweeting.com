Harmonic Analysis

Brandon Sweeting

NSF MPS-Ascend Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Department of Mathematics, Washington University in St. Louis

I work in real-variable harmonic analysis, studying weighted norm inequalities for singular integral operators, maximal operators, and the Bergman projection, with dyadic and Bellman function techniques.
